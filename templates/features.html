What's Inside

No clutter, no accounts you don't need, no noise.

Data

  • Live price fetching — pulled symbol by symbol from a real market data provider.
  • Market-hours awareness — the app knows when markets are closed and won't pretend stale data is fresh.
  • Graceful failure handling — a bad symbol or a rate limit doesn't take down the whole run.

Delivery

  • Automated daily email — sent every morning without needing to open the app.
  • Clean digest formatting — aligned, scannable, no HTML clutter.

Interface

  • Minimal live dashboard — a simple page to check prices on demand, outside of the email.
  • Focused stock list — a hand-picked set of companies, not an overwhelming universe of tickers.
